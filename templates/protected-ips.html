Keep these IPs unblocked on all enabled devices. If a protected IP appears in Shuns, the service queues its removal.

Turning protection off does not block the IP again.

About Protected IPs
Protection on
After each successful device check, the service queues removal of any block for this IP. Saving does not unblock it immediately; follow progress in Queue.
Protection off
The address stays in this list, but automatic unblocking stops. FTD may block it again. Deleting the entry also stops automatic unblocking.
Previously imported IPs
Turning protection on also turns off auto-restore for this IP on every device. Turning protection off does not turn auto-restore back on. To have the service keep the IP blocked again, disable protection and create a new import.

Turning protection off or deleting an entry cancels automatic unblock requests that have not started. A running command finishes; manually requested unblocks are not cancelled.

Anyone signed in can edit this list. If someone else saves changes while you are editing, reload the list and review their changes before saving again. Reloading discards your unsaved edits.
