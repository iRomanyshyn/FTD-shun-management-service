What does pausing do?

Existing blocks stay on the devices and continue to be monitored. Removals and Protected-IP automatic unblocking continue. An operation already in progress finishes and is checked.

Imports pauses initial application; automatic restoration pauses re-adding previously applied or observed blocks. Saved imports and rules are retained, not cancelled.

The two pauses are independent. Resuming uses a fresh device check before scheduling missing addresses; it does not clear errors or replay unknown commands.
